      Questions worth asking before you buy

      • Is it ready or off-plan — and what is the handover date written in the contract?
      • What are the yearly service charges and other running costs?
      • If you are shown a rental figure: is it an actual signed lease, or only an estimate?
      • Can buyers of your nationality own property in this area, and on what basis?
      • How long is the daily drive to work or school at rush hour?
      • Is the advisor licensed, and have you checked the licence yourself?

      What we are — and are not

      We are an introduction service for property buyers. We are not a real-estate broker, lawyer or financial adviser, and nothing here is legal, financial or investment advice.

      We do not publish prices, availability, discounts or expected returns. Always confirm those — and the advisor's licence — directly with the advisor or developer.

      If you buy through an advisor we introduced you to, we may receive a referral fee from that advisor or developer. You do not pay us anything.
