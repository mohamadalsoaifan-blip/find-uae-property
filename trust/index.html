Trust Center

Everything you need to judge whether to trust us, in one place. Where a legal question is not settled yet, we say so and keep the related feature switched off.

Last reviewed: 2026-10-04

Our role

We are an introduction and decision-support service for property and cars in the UAE.

We are not a real-estate broker, developer, car dealer, lender, insurer, lawyer or financial adviser. We do not sell, list or price property or cars, and we never take a deposit or a payment from you.

Every price, specification, fee, permit and contract term must be confirmed in writing by the licensed company you deal with.

Legal questions and their status

These are the regulated areas that touch our service, and where each one stands today.

  • Property advertisements We do not do this
    We publish no property listings or offers. In Dubai a property advertisement needs a valid Dubai Land Department (Trakheesi) permit; if we ever show one, it will carry its permit number, and without a valid permit it will not be shown.
  • Property brokerage We do not do this
    We do not negotiate, show units or handle contracts. That is licensed brokerage work, done only by the licensed advisor you choose.
  • Referral fees on property Under legal review
    Whether we may receive a referral fee from a licensed property advisor is being checked with a UAE lawyer. Until it is confirmed, we do not collect or record any such fee.
  • Marketing calls and messages We do not do this
    We do not make marketing calls or send marketing messages. We reply only to people who asked us to, through the channel they chose.
  • Personal data (UAE PDPL) In place
    Separate consents, minimum data, no ID or bank details, deletion on request, and an audit trail of every consent and change.
  • Car sales and finance We do not do this
    We do not sell cars or arrange finance. The calculators explain the maths with your own numbers; the dealer and the bank confirm the real figures.

How our recommendations work

Suggestions come from fixed, published rules applied to your own answers (budget, needs, timing). The same answers always give the same result.

Whether a partner pays us, and how much, is never part of the ranking. We show what fits you first.

We never present an estimate as a fact, and we never promise a price, return, approval or availability.

What our data labels mean

Every number we show belongs to one of these kinds:

Verified
Taken from an official source, linked, with the date we last checked it.
Partner-provided
Given by a licensed partner; they are responsible for it, so confirm it with them.
Estimated
Calculated by us from stated rules; a guide, not a quote.
Your input
A number you entered. We use it as you gave it.
Unknown
We do not have it. We say so rather than guess.

How we are paid

You never pay us.

A licensed partner may pay us a referral fee under a written agreement if you go ahead with them. This never changes the price you pay, and for property it applies only after legal confirmation (see above).

Your privacy choices

Three separate choices, never pre-ticked: being contacted about your own request; sharing your request with one licensed partner; and marketing. We do not send marketing, so we never ask for the third.

Saying no to sharing does not stop us from answering you. You can withdraw any choice, or ask us to delete everything, at any time.

We never ask for your ID, passport, bank or card details, passwords or one-time codes.
