Questions to ask any setup provider

  1. Is my exact activity on your licence activity list?
  2. What is the total first-year cost (licence, visas, establishment card, workspace) — and what renews every year?
  3. How many residence visas does the package allow, and what workspace does each visa need?
  4. Can this licence invoice clients in the UAE mainland? If not, what is the route, and what does it cost?
  5. What help do you give with opening a business bank account?
  6. If the application is refused, what is refunded?
  7. Which tax registrations will my company need?

Why no prices or timelines? They depend on the authority, the activity and the package, and they change. We do not guess them. Ask providers for a written, all-in quote.
